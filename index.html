About Me

I’m an AI/ML Engineer with a BS in Electrical Engineering from Namal University. My work spans Generative AI, computer vision, machine learning, and embedded systems, connecting models with applications, data processing, hardware, and infrastructure.

At T8 Launchpad, I work with RAG pipelines, Linux-based application environments, Docker, and deployment workflows. My projects range from document assistants and image-analysis applications to sensor-based recognition and Raspberry Pi monitoring.

Alongside MLOps and cloud engineering, I’m interested in running LLMs locally, Stable Diffusion and diffusion-based image generation, and GPU-accelerated inference. I want to explore model optimization and efficient inference on Linux-based and edge AI systems further. I welcome engineering roles, freelance work, and technical collaborations.
